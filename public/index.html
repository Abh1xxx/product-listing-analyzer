How the score works

Five components contribute up to 20 points each. This is a project-defined educational metric, with no validation against marketplace ranking, human ratings, or sales.

Title completeness
20 for at least 5 words; 12 for 3–4; 5 for 1–2; 0 if empty.
Keyword coverage
20 × the fraction of extracted corpus-TF-IDF keywords that appear in the title.
Description quality
Up to 8 for 30+ words (4 for 15–29), 6 for 2+ sentences (3 for 1), and 6 for 10+ unique words (3 for 5–9).
Attribute coverage
20 × detected attribute categories ÷ categories supported by dataset columns. Detection uses phrase matching against corpus values.
Repetition and readability
Start at 20. Repeated-token ratios above 5%, 10%, or 20% subtract 2, 5, or 10 points. A title over 150 characters and a sentence over 35 words each subtract 5.

What to keep in mind

TF-IDF measures corpus rarity and word overlap, not meaning. Attribute rules can miss synonyms. Porter stems may not be dictionary words; WordNet lemmas use noun forms by default. Sentence splitting can misread abbreviations.
